Colors

A card’s colors are four different questions: whose deck it is, what ink it is printed in, what a rule thinks it is — and, on a court, none of those. The first three come apart on exactly one card.

The back is ink on transparency, so the fill behind it is the whole of what a deck color changes — turn the cards over and six decks are one deck. A court is a painting rather than a pip, and takes its colors from the deck’s palette instead of the suit’s ink; all seven are at courts.
