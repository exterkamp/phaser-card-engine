phaser-card-engine

The shared half of two card games, demonstrated one primitive at a time. Every page below is a real Phaser scene built out of the package, and nothing else.

  • Stacks

    One of every direction a pile can run, in both draw orders — and a card you can drag from one to another. Stack.

  • Hands

    Cards held rather than stacked: they pivot around the grip, so the fan bulges up in the middle. Hand.

  • Throwing

    Tap the felt to throw a card at that spot, or tap a pile to throw one onto it. throwCard.

  • Dealing hold'em

    Four seats dealt automatically, the way a dealer deals it. Every primitive at once, and no rules at all.

  • Courts

    The twelve court cards, recolored while the page is running. Mix a deck nobody baked. renderCourts.

  • Colors

    Whose deck it is, what ink it is printed in, what a rule thinks it is, and what a court does instead. colorOf.

  • Card sizes

    The same card at seven widths, drawn at each size rather than drawn once and scaled. cardFaceMetrics.
A card game is tested with a thumb — npm run demo prints a LAN address alongside the local one, and that is the one to open on a phone.
