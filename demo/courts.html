Courts

Every court in the package comes out of twelve SVG files, recoloured and rasterised here. The seven presets used to be seven directories of baked WebP — 4.1MB of it — and they are three hex values each now, which is all they ever were. Mix an eighth.

Black and paper stay put: black is the mass the line work sits on, and paper has to match the card underneath or the art reads as a sticker.
